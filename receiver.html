Microphone is off. Meeting mode requires the PC virtual audio cable and trusted HTTPS.
  1. Keep the PC and phone on the same private Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.
  2. Select your earphones as the phone's media output.
  3. Connect, then play music on your PC. If needed, tap Play above.
  4. For meetings, enable the microphone above. In the desktop meeting app choose CABLE Output as microphone and your normal Windows speakers as speaker output.
