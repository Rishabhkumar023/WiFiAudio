Listen in a few steps

  1. Pair your Bluetooth earphones with your Android phone.
  2. Connect the PC and phone to the same private Wi-Fi, or connect the PC to your phone's hotspot.
  3. Extract the ZIP and run WiFiAudio.exe on the PC.
  4. Scan the PC's QR code with the phone. Open it in Chrome and tap Connect audio.
  5. Play YouTube or other audio on the PC. Keep the phone page open and awake.

Speak in meetings

Meeting mode can send your phone's microphone back to a Windows virtual microphone.

  1. Install VB-CABLE from its publisher, then follow its restart instructions. Enable Windows microphone access.
  2. Run Start-Meeting.cmd. Follow its one-time Android certificate setup for secure microphone access.
  3. On the phone, enable Send my microphone and allow microphone permission.
  4. In your meeting app select CABLE Output as the microphone. Keep normal Windows speakers as the speaker output.

What to expect from this preview

Desktop Chrome audio, microphone return through a real VB-CABLE recording endpoint, mute and reconnect have been tested with synthetic audio. Android phones, physical earbuds and individual meeting apps still need device testing.

Your browser may use the phone mic instead of the earbuds mic. Bluetooth adds delay; background and screen-off playback are not guaranteed.

Does this page stream my PC audio?

No. This is a download page. Audio runs between the app on your own Windows PC and your phone on the local network. Share this download link with friends, not your private pairing QR or certificate keys.
